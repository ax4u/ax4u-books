5. 상괭이 / East Asian narrow-ridged finless porpoise
Neophocaena asiaeorientalis — marine subspecies N. a. sunameri.
Original geometry, five-bone swimming rig, procedural grey skin and fine facial details by GOBIBI × SANG#MUNG production. No downloaded/recoloured whale mesh or photographic texture was used. This is an original anatomical interpretation, not a scanned specimen.
Taxonomic convention: Society for Marine Mammalogy, April 2026 list. Some other sources use Neophocaena sunameri as a species; this production explicitly follows the SMM subspecies treatment.
Anatomical references checked 2026-09-09:
https://marinemammalscience.org/science-and-publications/list-marine-mammal-species-subspecies/
https://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/xhtml/03B887D96B2EFFB8FAA27A34FD7D8FF6
https://porpoise.org/about-porpoises/narrow-ridged-finless-porpoise/
No triangular dorsal fin or dolphin-like projecting beak; narrow dorsal ridge, rounded forehead, subdued mouth crease, grey body and paler underside. Sculpted proportions are an interpretation, not measured proportions of an identified Korean specimen.

Movement and collision simulation:
11 independent bodies, including mobile. Bodies share a consistent world scale, with representative visual lengths rather than population averages or maximum records.
Independent broadside cruising and gradual offscreen turns use changing velocities and inertia. There are no synchronized sprints or automatic eye closeups. Predictive avoidance is followed by actual mass-weighted collision impulses and restitution when conservative 3D capsule proxies meet. Collision capsules enclose the animated body/fin/tail envelope; they are simplified safety volumes, not exact deformable tissue or fluid-dynamics simulation. Bodies are not intentionally allowed to pass through one another. A fixed observer and different physical depths create close and distant views. On viewport changes the swimming bounds and projected composition are reflowed together; model geometry is never stretched.
Animation update rate and pixel resolution may be reduced on slow devices without silently reducing the scene's population. Animals outside the camera view remain simulated.
